A little clarity
goes a long way.

Commonplace is a working, private research desk. Read documents, discover shared language, collect exact passages, add your interpretation, and take your work with you.

Local by design

Documents and notes stay in this browser’s local storage. Nothing is uploaded. This is device-local storage, not encryption or cloud backup. Export important work.

Connections you can inspect

Related sources use TF-IDF cosine similarity. Shared words explain suggestions. This is lexical discovery, not semantic AI or fact checking.

A source for every quote

Saved passages keep the exact text, title, author, and original line numbers. Your notes stay separate. Sample documents are fictional and labeled.

Bring your own material

Import UTF-8 TXT, Markdown, CSV, or JSON. Up to 2 MB per file and 8 MB of source text per workspace, subject to browser storage limits. PDF is not supported.

/ Search⌘ / Ctrl K SearchEsc Close a source

Restore a backup through “Add to your library.” A Commonplace backup merges its documents and saved passages into the current workspace. The document text is treated as content, never as executable HTML.
